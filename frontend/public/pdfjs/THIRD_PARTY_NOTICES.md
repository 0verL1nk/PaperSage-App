# PDF.js browser assets

These assets are copied from `pdfjs-dist` 5.4.296, distributed under Apache-2.0. The full license is in `LICENSE`; the CMap and standard-font directories include their upstream notices and font-specific licenses.

They are served locally because PDF.js needs Adobe CMaps to extract and render CID-encoded CJK text, and standard font data for some PDF documents. Source: https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js/tree/v5.4.296
